**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Car Wash Simulator**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life enhancements for Car Wash Simulator, including money, experience, respect points, timed-job support, and cleaning-task assistance. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the game normally and load into a car wash or district. The trainer does not replace movement controls, driving, or interaction keys. Activate enhancements after the player has entered the game world and avoid toggling features during loading screens.

### Tools & Inventory

Use money and progression options to support purchases, equipment upgrades, property operations, and contract preparation. Inventory-related changes should be activated while the relevant management or contract interface is open, then disabled before changing locations.

### World & Environment

Car Wash Simulator includes the Rent Hike Ridge and Havenwood districts, ten car washes, six cars, a day-and-night cycle, random event cards, timed jobs, driveable vehicles, and plaza mini-games. Use the trainer to explore business expansion and available activities at your own pace.

### Combat & Weapons

The game has no conventional combat system. Its action-focused activity is a plaza shooting mini-game involving flying targets, using a toy pistol. Trainer options should be treated as mini-game assistance rather than combat enhancements.
